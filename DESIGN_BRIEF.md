# Hindsight
A market journal for people aged 16–28 learning to connect a price move with an event or a decision of their own. Lead with a question a reader could ask out loud. Give them the date, the answer and a way to inspect the evidence.

## Composition
Today opens on one short market observation in a frosted reading panel. A narrow date margin anchors the text. Past the panel’s right edge, the reader sees the cabin across a clearing. On phones the clearing becomes a shallow panorama above the same reading panel.
Keep the cabin, ridge and tree line fixed across all twelve paintings. Cluster texture in the near branches, soften the distant ridge and leave open sky at the reading edge. Warm window light marks the cabin at every hour. Paint each scene from an original sketch. At night, group pines into broad silhouettes and soften their edges with distance. Keep stars sparse near the reading panel and the window glow dimmer than the primary control.
Give each route one focal element. Today has the recap, Markets the selected chart, Review the selected sale, Learn the current illustration. Secondary numbers sit in aligned rows with units and dates.
Use one tinted glass surface over scenery and opaque nested disclosures. Limit accents to the primary action and the current data series. Reserve up/down arrows and signed values for measured changes.
Draw 20 px open-stroke icons on a 2 px grid: horizon for Today, plotted points for Markets, a folded receipt for Review and an open book for Learn. Pair each with text.

## Navigation and products
Four persistent text tabs sit beneath the desktop masthead and at the bottom on phones. Preserve route history, active tab and focus on return. Settings opens from the masthead.
- Today — Dated recap, index moves and a link to supporting news. Local weather opens hourly detail, feels-like temperature, sunrise and sunset.
- Markets — Search, watchlist and one persistent chart. Open overview, symbol detail, calendar, screener and ticker tape on demand.
- News — Reach from Today or Markets. Filter by category, read one sentence on relevance, then follow the timestamped source.
- Review — Try example trades or import a local file. Broker instructions precede mapping. Results join sale timelines, fees and habit checks with private PNG and CSV exports.
- Learn — Seven illustrated price-movement lessons. A term hint returns readers to the same chart, date or trade after reading.
- Settings and About — City, units, scene preferences, local data controls, methodology, contact, privacy and versioned terms with a decline path.

## Five improvements over Prolepsis
1. Give visitors a dated market observation on arrival. Put research caveats beside the claim they qualify.
2. Show one selected symbol and its comparison period. Open the wider symbol universe on request.
3. Place a keyboard-accessible definition beside the first use of a term such as “index”, with one numerical example.
4. Connect a recap sentence to its supporting timestamped source and mark cached figures with their observation date.
5. Let a reader follow one sale through matching, fees and later prices, with assumptions visible beside the result.

## Tokens
Scene tint by season, ordered day / sunset / night:
- Winter — frost #DCE7E6 / rose clay #DBAE9A / slate #25384A
- Spring — sage #DCE8CB / apricot #EDBD9D / pine #203C38
- Summer — straw #F1E6B9 / honey #E5AD79 / indigo #29354E
- Autumn — oat #E8D5AE / copper #CA9379 / plum #3B303D
Day and sunset use ink #242B27 on cream #FFF9EF at 94% opacity. Night uses #F7F1E6 on charcoal #202925 at 96%. Use solid versions behind charts and on devices without blur.
Primary action uses spruce #285649 with white text, or pale mint #BCE4D0 with dark ink at night. Plot labels include sign, unit and date. Check contrast against all twelve paintings.
Use Spectral for recap and lesson headings, DM Sans for controls and tabular figures. Type sizes 13, 16, 20, 28 and 40 px. Body leading 1.55, reading measure 60 characters.
Spacing 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 and 48 px. Page gutters 16 px mobile and 32 px desktop. Corners 12 px for controls and 20 px for panels. Shadow 0 8px 24px rgba(31,38,33,.12).
Only scene crossfade (420 ms) and disclosure fade (140 ms). Reduced motion sets both to zero and freezes ticker movement. Use static skeletons, visible 3 px focus rings and 44 px touch targets.
During outages show the last successful observation and its time. An empty watchlist offers symbol search. Missing review prices leave individual comparisons unavailable while matched sales and fees remain readable.
