Step 1

Import your trades

Your file is read in this browser and is never uploaded. Hindsight pairs sales with earlier purchases first in, first out (FIFO) to find completed trades. Plain-text files only, up to 2 MB and 20,000 data rows.

Where to find a file: in your broker’s account history or activity page, look for an export or download option (usually CSV). Include both buy and sell transactions.

Optional

Live market prices

Hindsight fetches weekly prices directly from Alpha Vantage using your own free API key. Free keys are limited (about 25 requests a day), so each ticker costs one request. Symbols must match Alpha Vantage’s format; non-US listings usually need an exchange suffix.

Use a free key only. Your key stays in this browser’s localStorage and is sent only to Alpha Vantage when you fetch prices. Never paste a paid, shared, or company key. Hindsight never logs it.

About & privacy

Your trades stay yours.

Everything Hindsight does happens in this browser tab. Your trade file is parsed on your device and is never uploaded. There is no backend, no account, no analytics, and no third-party script on this page.

The only network request the app can make is to https://www.alphavantage.co when you choose to fetch live prices. That request goes directly from your browser to Alpha Vantage and includes the ticker you asked for and the free API key you entered. Alpha Vantage can see your IP address, as it would with any direct request.

Hindsight stores a small amount of data in your browser: your API key (only if you paste one) and cached weekly price series so you do not spend a request twice. It does not store your imported trades. Use the data controls below to remove that data.

Not financial advice. Hindsight is an educational review tool. It does not recommend buying or selling any security. Past results do not predict future returns.

Your data on this device
