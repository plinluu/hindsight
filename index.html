How it works

Three steps. No setup.

The chart above is live. Click a ticker or change the window to see another trade. With your own file, it goes like this.

  1. 01

    Export your trades

    Every broker has an export button on its activity page. A CSV, TSV, or a copied table all work.

  2. 02

    Paste it in

    The file is read in this tab and never uploaded. Hindsight pairs each sale with the shares you bought earlier, and you check the columns before anything is matched.

  3. 03

    Read the chart

    The white line is the price. The shaded block is what the shares were worth after you sold. Scroll on for fees, habits, and a card you can share.

No account, and no key needed for the demo. Live prices are optional and use your own free Alpha Vantage key.

01 · Left on the table

What you gave up by selling

The gap between your sell price and where the stock went after. The chart above follows one trade. This ranks all of them, worst first, then adds up your running result sale by sale.

02 · Fees

What the broker kept

Commissions and currency conversion, added up across every completed trade. A file with no fee column shows zero here. You can point Hindsight at the right column during import.

03 · Habits

Three patterns worth checking

Chasing a move, holding losers longer than winners, and trading again right after a loss. A pattern only appears when the numbers in your file back it up.

04 · Import

Bring your own trades

Most brokers have an export button on their activity page. CSV, TSV, or TXT all work, and so does pasting the table. Hindsight guesses the columns and lets you correct it before anything is matched.

Live prices are optional. Without them the charts stay empty and everything else still works.

How matching works

Optional

Live market prices

Prices come straight from Alpha Vantage with your own free key. A free key allows about 25 requests a day, and each ticker costs one. Symbols use Alpha Vantage's format; non-US listings usually need an exchange suffix.

Use a free key only. It stays in this browser and is sent only to Alpha Vantage when you fetch. Never paste a paid, shared, or company key. Hindsight does not log it.

06 · Privacy

Your file stays in this tab

No account, no server, no analytics, no cookie, no third-party script. That is the whole design, not a setting you have to find.

There is no backend and no account. Trades you import are processed in this tab, not uploaded to me, and not saved. A refresh brings the demo back.

The only request the app can make goes to https://www.alphavantage.co, and only when you click Fetch prices. That request carries the ticker, the key you entered, and your IP address, the same as any direct request. A key you paste is saved in this browser as hs_key; weekly prices are cached as hs_s_TICKER for 12 hours so you do not spend a request twice.

To remove them, use Forget my key or Delete all my data in the card beside this text, or clear site data for this page in your browser settings. No cookies, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party scripts. Nothing loads from another domain except the Alpha Vantage request you trigger.

The waitlist button only opens a third-party hosted form when one is configured. That provider would receive whatever email you type and handle it under its own privacy policy. Right now no form is connected, so the button says so and no email is collected here.

Not financial advice. Hindsight is an educational review tool. It does not recommend buying or selling anything. Past results do not predict future ones.

What is stored on this device

08 · Risk

Not financial advice and risk

The short version: this looks backward, and looking backward is easy.

Hindsight is a historical review. It pairs trades you already made with prices from after each sale. It does not forecast, rate or suggest a trade.

Hindsight bias is the reason this tool exists and the reason to be careful with it. Once you know what a price did, the right move seems obvious. At the time it was not. Every number here describes the past. A clean-looking chart is not a lesson about the future.

Investing can lose money. Prices fall as well as rise, and a sale that looks early today can look lucky or late next month. Nothing on this page changes that.

The demo is made up. Its trades and prices are simulated. Do not read them as a real portfolio or a real result.

Your results are only as good as your file. Wrong dates, missing fees, split-adjusted prices or a mismatched column will move every number on the page. Check the import before you draw conclusions.
